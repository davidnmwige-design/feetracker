Elimu Pay — Production-Hardening Progress Report

Prepared by: Datacare Limited For: David Njiru, Elimu Pay Date: 17 June 2026 Contact: info@datacare.co.ke · www.datacare.co.ke · 0722 155 752

1. Executive summary

Work to take Elimu Pay from a working build to a launch-ready, production-grade platform is well advanced and on track for the agreed launch window. We now have full access to the codebase and database, and have completed the security, stability, and core "market-readiness" work — the items a school and a regulator would expect of a system that handles real money and the personal data of children.

Every change has been delivered into a single, reviewed change-set, and each piece has been tested against a large, realistic copy of the data (thousands of students and payments) before being accepted. The platform is materially more secure, faster under load, and now supports the payment and communication features that schools asked for.

A small amount of work remains — chiefly a visual/usability refresh and a few items that depend on information only you or the schools can provide (payment and messaging account details). These are listed in Sections 5 and 6.

2. What we have completed

Security and data protection

  • Closed the highest-risk security gaps around payment integrity and login, so that

fake payments cannot be injected and login/2-factor sessions cannot be forged.

  • Strengthened how sensitive data is stored and how automated/background jobs are

authorised, and removed a diagnostic endpoint that should never reach production.

  • Independent penetration-test findings supplied earlier were reconciled against the

live system; the outstanding high-risk items have been addressed.

Reliability and scale

  • Re-engineered the database connection approach so the platform stays responsive as the

number of schools grows (essential for a multi-school, cloud deployment).

  • Added an automated safety net of 38 tests plus a large-scale stress test that we run

before accepting any change, so regressions are caught early.

Fee collection and compliance

  • Tax-compliant invoice numbering. Invoices now carry unique, sequential numbers

assigned by the system — a requirement for KRA-compliant record-keeping. This was proven correct even when many invoices are produced at the same moment.

  • Instant M-Pesa payment requests (STK Push). Staff can now prompt a parent's phone

to pay directly; a confirmed payment is recorded and reconciled to the right student automatically, and the parent receives a confirmation.

  • Faster, server-generated invoices. Invoice PDFs are now produced by the server

rather than the staff member's browser, so sending invoices — including in bulk — no longer slows down or freezes the screen.

Parent communication

  • Real SMS fee reminders via a Kenyan bulk-SMS gateway, alongside the existing email

and WhatsApp options, including an automated scheduled reminder run.

Performance and pre-launch tidy-up

  • Fixed the heaviest screen in the app (the reminders list) so it stays fast even with

thousands of students.

  • Resolved a class of display/console warnings that affected polish and correctness.
  • Removed the public "demo" area ahead of the live launch.

4. Key findings

  • The new fee-collection and compliance features are correct and safe. The most

important property — that money and invoice numbers are handled accurately even under simultaneous activity — was demonstrated, not assumed.

  • Security posture is strong. Payment-confirmation channels reject anything they

cannot verify, and sensitive actions are limited to authorised staff.

  • Performance is good and will be better in production. The biggest gains came from

only loading what is shown on screen; one heavy screen has been brought into line with the rest. (Measured speeds during testing are slower than the live system will be, because the test environment runs in an un-optimised development mode.)

  • A few usability/polish items remain and are itemised below; none are blockers.

5. What remains

  1. Visual and usability refresh — a consistent design pass and accessibility improvements across the screens. We have deliberately held back front-end changes pending your written go-ahead; this is the main remaining body of work and can begin on your word.
  2. Two small front-end additions already approved — an in-app button to trigger an M-Pesa payment request, and a one-click invoice download — to be wired next.
  3. Final compliance items — a published data-protection/privacy position aligned to Kenya's Data Protection Act.

6. What we need from you / the schools

To switch the new features fully "on" in production, we will need:

  • Bulk-SMS account (sender name) so SMS reminders can send under the school's identity.
  • M-Pesa details per school — each school's own paybill/till and the matching M-Pesa

online passkey. Per your direction, every school uses its own M-Pesa number for its collections; the platform is built around that model.

  • Email sending credentials for invoice and payment-confirmation emails.

We can provide a short, plain checklist for each of these.

7. Outlook

The platform is in a strong, launch-ready position on security, stability, compliance, and core features. The remaining work is primarily the visual/usability refresh and switching on the third-party accounts above. We remain confident in the agreed launch window and will share a final go-live checklist as we approach it.

This report summarises progress and outcomes. Technical implementation detail is held in the project's source control and is available to your team on request.

Elimu Pay — Go-Live Setup Guide

Prepared by: Datacare Limited · For: David Njiru, Elimu Pay · Date: 17 June 2026 Contact: info@datacare.co.ke · www.datacare.co.ke · 0722 155 752

This guide walks you through everything that must be set up on your side to move Elimu Pay into the live (production) environment. Each section says what to do, the rough cost, and exactly what to send back to us so we can connect it.

Prefer us to handle all of it? If you'd rather not do these steps yourself, Datacare can take care of the entire production setup end-to-end — registering and configuring every account (domain, hosting, database, email, SMS, M-Pesa), wiring it all up, and deploying — for a one-time setup fee of KES 20,000. This is a separate, optional service and is not part of the original arrangement. (Third-party charges above — domain, hosting, SMS units, etc. — are billed by those providers and are not included in this fee.)

Important — ownership. Please create each account in Elimu Pay's name (your business email and your payment card). You own the accounts and the billing; we only need the access keys to connect them. Where an account supports it, you can instead add us as a team member (we'll give you the email to invite). Never let a vendor account be owned by an individual contractor.

How to send us credentials — securely. Do not send keys over plain email, SMS, or WhatsApp. Use one of these:

  1. A one-time secret link — go to onetimesecret.com, paste the value, send us the link (it self-destructs after we open it), or
  2. Share via a password manager (we can use a shared 1Password/Bitwarden vault), or
  3. Read them to us on a call while we enter them.

Step 1 — Buy a domain name

What to do

  1. Decide the address. We suggest elimupay.co.ke (Kenyan, trusted locally).
  2. Register it at simbahost.co.ke — search for the name and check out (KES 1,500/year).
  3. After purchase, you'll get a login to the registrar's DNS / domain management page. We will give you a short list of DNS records to add (for the website, email, etc.), or you can add us as a user on the registrar so we configure it.

What to send us

  • The domain name(s) you bought, and access to the DNS settings (invite us, or be

available to paste in the 4–6 records we provide).

We connect your new domain (Step 1) to the existing Vercel deployment so the app answers at https://elimupay.co.ke instead of the temporary address.

Step 2 — Set up email sending (Resend)

This powers invoice emails, payment confirmations, and email reminders.

What to do

  1. Go to resend.com → Sign Up (free tier: 3,000 emails/month, 100/day).
  2. In the left menu, open Domains → Add Domain and enter your domain (e.g. elimupay.co.ke). Resend will show you 3–4 DNS records (SPF, DKIM, and a return-path).
  3. Add those records in your domain's DNS page from Step 1 (or send them to us to add). Wait for Resend to show the domain as Verified (usually minutes, sometimes a few hours).
  4. Open API Keys → Create API Key, name it "Elimu Pay Production", and copy the key (it starts with re_…). You can only see it once — copy it immediately.

What to send us (securely)

  • The Resend API key (re_…).
  • Confirmation that your domain shows Verified in Resend.
  • The "from" address you want parents to see (e.g. fees@elimupay.co.ke or

no-reply@elimupay.co.ke).

Note: the app currently emails via a Google account. We will switch it to Resend (a small change on our side) so that, once you send the key above, everything sends under your own domain with better deliverability.

Step 3 — Set up SMS (Celcom Africa)

What to do

  1. Visit celcomafrica.com and register for a Bulk SMS account (or email/call their sales team — they often onboard you directly).
  2. Ask them to register a Sender ID for you — a short text name parents will see as the sender, e.g. ELIMUPAY or your school brand. (Sender-ID approval goes through the Communications Authority and can take 1–2 weeks, so start this early. There is a small one-time registration fee.)
  3. Buy SMS units to load your account (start small, e.g. KES 2,000–5,000).
  4. From your Celcom account dashboard, obtain your API key, Partner ID, and the approved Sender ID (shortcode).

What to send us (securely)

  • API key, Partner ID, and the approved Sender ID.

Until these arrive, the SMS feature stays safely dormant (it simply does nothing).

Step 4 — Set up M-Pesa (Safaricom Daraja) — per school

Per your direction, each school collects to its own M-Pesa paybill/till. There are two parts: a one-time developer setup (platform level), and a per-school detail.

A. One-time (platform) — Daraja developer account

  1. Go to developer.safaricom.co.ke → Sign up with the Elimu Pay email.
  2. Create an App (it gives you a Consumer Key and Consumer Secret). These let the platform talk to M-Pesa on schools' behalf.

B. Per school — their M-Pesa details For each school joining the platform you (or the school) will need:

  1. The school's Paybill or Till number (their Lipa na M-Pesa business shortcode).
  2. The school's M-Pesa "online" passkey for that shortcode (Safaricom issues this when the shortcode is enabled for online payments / "Lipa na M-Pesa Online"). This is required for the instant payment prompt (STK Push) feature.
  3. We register the school's paybill inside the app so real-time payment notifications flow in.

Going live: Daraja starts in a sandbox (test) mode. When you're ready, Safaricom runs a short go-live approval to switch your app to production. We handle the technical side; you may be asked to confirm business details.

What to send us (securely)

  • The Consumer Key and Consumer Secret from your Daraja app.
  • For each school: Paybill/Till and the online passkey.

Behind-the-scenes secrets (we handle these)

A few internal secrets (for encrypting data, securing automated jobs, and authenticating M-Pesa callbacks) are generated by us and stored only in the hosting environment. You don't need to do anything here — they're already set in the current deployment.
